The reservation form is a browser-local portfolio demonstration.
The initial March 2025 month follows the design; weekday placement and month
lengths use the Gregorian calendar, including leap years.
Standalone browser and operating-system status bars are supplied by the viewing device.
The portfolio phone model renders status chrome in a 402-point coordinate system,
visually checked against the local iPhone 17 Pro simulator. The same scale drives
its safe-area inset. The page fills that inset only once its menu becomes sticky.
Mobile buttons have at least 44px touch targets. Menu ingredient text is 10px
instead of the source's 7px, with flowing layout to accommodate the text.
